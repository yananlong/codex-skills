# Decision Gates Template

Use this template to force the plan to stop, revise, or proceed based on real evidence rather than inertia.

| Gate ID | Opens after | Decision question | Proceed if | Revise if | Stop if | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | B1 | Does the anchor result support the primary claim? | | `add_evidence`: | | |

Start each "Revise if" cell with one repair from the suite vocabulary (`add_evidence`, `sharpen_comparator`, `lower_assurance`, `reframe`, `narrow`, `pivot`, `stop`) followed by a colon and the condition. List repairs that keep the claim before `narrow`.
