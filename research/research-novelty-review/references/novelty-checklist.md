# Novelty Checklist

- What exactly is claimed to be new?
- Which part is the strongest contribution: framing, method, protocol, artifact, or result?
- Which prior works are closest, not just vaguely related?
- What is the single strongest overlap that could kill the novelty claim?
- Would a skeptical reviewer call this recombination, renaming, routine scaling, or a benchmark reshuffle?
- Where does the claim land on the 1-5 decision scale in `decision-scale.md` (1 abandon, 2 major reframe, 3 narrow proceed, 4 proceed with careful positioning, 5 strong novelty position)?
- What is the narrowest defensible positioning that survives every kill-shot objection?
- What is the strongest supportable positioning the evidence still allows? Narrow only as far as a named overlap forces, not past it.
