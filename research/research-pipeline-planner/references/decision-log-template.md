# Decision Log Template

## Entry

- Date:
- Stage:
- Decision:
- Status: proceed / revise / stop
- Context:
- Alternatives considered:
- Rationale:
- Expected consequence:
- Cost if wrong: <what rework or loss a wrong call produces, so the reader can weigh it>
- Follow-up trigger:

## Ruling line

When a decision is made mid-task without a human (an ambiguity resolved, a conflict between two artifacts settled, a cap exceeded), record it as one line so it survives a context reset and reaches the human in the final report:

`Ruling: <what was decided> | <why> | <cost if wrong>`

An unrecorded deviation is a decision made in secret. Collect every ruling line into the handoff or final message.
